Privacy Policy

Updated: 10 October 2026.

Document processing

Selected documents are processed in your browser. This build does not upload documents to PDFMivo. Reloading or closing the page clears the working session; downloaded files stay on your device.

Network requests and storage

Fonts and libraries come from Google Fonts and cdnjs. Those providers receive connection information such as your IP address. Hosting providers may record normal request logs. We store your light/dark theme preference in localStorage; clear site storage to remove it.

Advertising

Ad delivery is disabled in this release. If Google AdSense is enabled later, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other sites. Google advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalise advertising across sites.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center or visit aboutads.info for participating vendors. Applicable consent requirements must be implemented before advertising is enabled.

Contact and changes

Use the Contact page for the current support status. This policy will be updated when services, advertising or data practices change.
